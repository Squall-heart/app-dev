## Spider-Man: No Way Home

**Spider-Man: No Way Home** is one of my favorite superhero movies. It was released in 2021 and features Peter Parker as he faces unexpected challenges after his identity as Spider-Man becomes public.

## Movie Description

The movie follows Peter Parker as he asks Doctor Strange for help to make people forget his identity. However, the spell goes wrong and brings unexpected visitors from other universes.

### Main Characters

- **Peter Parker** - The main character and Spider-Man.
- **Doctor Strange** - A powerful sorcerer who helps Peter.
- **MJ** - Peter's girlfriend and close friend.
- **Ned Leeds** - Peter's best friend.

### Why I Like This Movie

I like this movie because:

1. It has an interesting and exciting story.
2. The action scenes are entertaining.
3. The characters have good chemistry.
4. It teaches lessons about friendship and responsibility.
5. It has emotional and memorable moments.
